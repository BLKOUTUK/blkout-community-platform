Mental health · Black gay, bi and trans men in the UK

Mental healthwithout community, there is no liberation

Our mental health is made in company: in belonging, in being seen, in having people who have our back. That is where BLKOUT starts.

Our approach

Connection first

We don't start from what is wrong with Black queer men. We start from what we already do for each other, and build on it: gatherings, conversation, making things together, people who check in. Culture is what we do, and doing it together is how things change.

We also know why the official picture is so thin. The system runs on data, and the data was never built to see us.

The system runs on data.The data doesn't see us.So the system doesn't serve us.

So we gather our own evidence, with consent, and make the case for mental health support that is designed with us, funded properly and accountable to the people it serves.

Our campaign

Critical Frequency

Critical Frequency is BLKOUT's mental health campaign. It sets out the evidence, the cost of doing nothing and a plan for change led by our community, and it asks the people who commission services to act on it.

If you need support today

Talk to someone now

BLKOUT is a community, not a crisis service. If you are struggling, these are free and confidential. In an emergency, call 999.

  • 116 123Samaritans, any time, day or night · samaritans.org
