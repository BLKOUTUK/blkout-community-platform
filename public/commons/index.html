// why now

Users,

Three things BLKOUT built around the same question — who are we not reaching — and all three costing less than one consultant's scoping document. They're the proof that a shared infrastructure for us is possible, not the thing itself. The thing itself is what we'd build together.

// the offer

Dreamcatcher,
in three tiers.

Dreamcatcher is BLKOUT's AI-tool evaluation engine — a council of named judges that reads any tool against your values and your reach. We're opening it to peer organisations in three tiers, each one unlocking another voice on the council.

it is decision-support, not a binding vote. tools you assess run independently in your stack. the only collective layer is the demand signal across orgs.

Tier 1 · Live now

Rivera Reach Judge

Try it.

free, instant, no intake.

Paste a tool URL. Rivera asks the reach question — who is this tool not for? Accessibility floor, privacy architecture, async vs sync, register, geographic reach, cost. Verdict in under a minute. Stand-alone honest answer.

Named after Sylvia Rivera —
the one who told STAR
y'all better quiet down.

Open Dreamcatcher →
Tier 2 · After the retreat

Baldwin · Murray · Rustin · Rivera

Tune it.

a council that knows you.

Short partner intake — your stack, your learning gaps, your values, your wishlist. The four judges then read every tool through your context, not BLKOUT's. Baldwin on lock-in. Murray on data sovereignty. Rustin on what'll actually deploy in your stack. Rivera on reach.

Honest current limit:
without the survey, three of four judges
are BLKOUT-rooted.
Tier 2 fixes that.

Opens after 8 May
Tier 3 · Cohort

Newton cross-corpus

Co-build.

when the same question shows up three times.

A fifth judge — Newton — reads across the corpus. When three or more orgs ask similar questions, Newton surfaces the co-build opportunity: three of you are looking at archiving — here's what shared would look like. Cohort opens once Tier 2 has gathered enough signal.

Named after Huey P. Newton —
survival programmes are
the political education.
