Chapter 1

The story so far

  • In July the board adopted the membership offer: free membership with a full vote and a reciprocal obligation, plus optional sustainer tiers at £3 and £10 a month that buy no extra vote.
  • Membership opens on Monday 28 December 2026, Joseph Beam Day. The AGM, working date Tuesday 24 November, announces its shape.
  • Being known to us is not the same as being a member who shapes and owns what we do.
  • We believe that when people get into the room, they stay. The problem is the way in, not the room. So we're testing a reframe of Making Ourselves From Scratch: four dinners with participatory workshop space between them, the speech work done in people's own time, and everyone at the table as a participant.

Chapter 5

A member's year

Invent one person who first hears about BLKOUT in January 2027. In about 150 words, tell their year: where they hear about us, the first thing they actually do, the moment they almost drift away, and what keeps them. Walk them from Awareness to Participation to Ownership, and let at least one of our doorways appear.

On Thursday we'll read some aloud and ask of each: what would we have to do for this story to come true?

Practice

Get Me to Talk

Twenty minutes of listening practice. You meet a guarded man from our world with a story worth hearing: twelve questions, scored as they land, then a full playback, and an optional task on confidentiality and safeguarding checked against our own policy. Copy the prompt into any AI and press send.

Don't name your real person, or include anything that identifies them, and check your chat service's settings on saving conversations.
