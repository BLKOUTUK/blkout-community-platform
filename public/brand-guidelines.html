🎯 3 Required Elements on EVERY Asset

Every BLKOUT-branded design must include these three elements:

1. Logo (Top-Left or Top-Center)

  • Minimum size: 80px
  • Recommended: 120-150px
  • Clear space: 20px around logo
  • Color: White logo on dark backgrounds

2. Byline (Bottom of Design)

The Black Queer Men's Liberation Collective

  • Font: Inter, 14-16px
  • Color: White (#FFFFFF)
  • Position: Centered, 30-40px from bottom

3. Website (Below Byline)

blkoutuk.com

  • Font: Inter, 14-16px
  • Color: Liberation Gold (#FFD700) — CRITICAL!
  • Position: Centered, directly below byline

✅ DO's & ❌ DON'Ts

✅ DO (Maintains Brand Integrity)

  • Black backgrounds (#000000)
  • Logo in top-left/center (small)
  • Footer with byline + website
  • Website in gold (#FFD700)
  • Rounded corners (12px+)
  • High contrast white/black
  • Joyful, celebratory mood
  • Pride color accents
  • Generous spacing

❌ DON'T (Breaks Brand)

  • White/light backgrounds
  • Centered/huge logo
  • Missing footer elements
  • White website URL (must be gold!)
  • Sharp/angular edges
  • Low contrast text
  • Stretch/distort logo
  • Change logo colors
  • Corporate/sterile aesthetics
  • Generic stock photos

🎯 Pre-Flight Checklist

Before finalizing any BLKOUT-branded design, verify:

  • Logo present in top-left or top-center?
  • Logo is 80px minimum (120-150px ideal)?
  • Logo has 20px clear space around it?
  • Background is pure black (#000000)?
  • Text is white (#FFFFFF)?
  • Footer includes "The Black Queer Men's Liberation Collective"?
  • Website "blkoutuk.com" is included?
  • Website is in gold color (#FFD700)?
  • Rounded corners on cards/buttons (12px+)?
  • Design feels joyful and celebratory?
  • High contrast throughout?

👤 About the Editor

Dr Rob Berkeley

Site Editor & Creator

Award-winning busybody and recovering academic, Dr Rob Berkeley leads the development of BLKOUT Media. He was director of the racial justice think-tank, Runnymede Trust (2009-14) — the youngest director in its fifty-year history — before a stint advising the BBC on audience accountability. He serves as Vice Chair of the Baring Foundation, Chair of the Doc Society Charitable Trust, and on the boards of Stanley Arts (SE25), Riverside Studios, and the Black Boy Joy Club CIC. Rob was awarded an MBE in 2015 for services to equality.
